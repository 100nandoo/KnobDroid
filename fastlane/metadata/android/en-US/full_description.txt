KnobDroid fixes a specific Android audio problem: some phones drive the Apple USB-C to 3.5 mm headphone adapter at a much lower hardware volume than expected.

The app watches for the dongle, requests USB permission when needed, and sends the hardware control command that restores normal output level.

Features:
- Detects the supported USB DAC automatically
- Applies the hardware volume fix after permission is granted
- Runs quietly in the background after setup
- Built with Jetpack Compose

Notes:
- Built primarily for the US Apple USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter
- May work with other UAC2 DACs that respond to the same command
- Requires a compatible USB audio dongle and Android device support
